\section*{Authors' Contributions}
Frieder Stolzenburg and Oliver Obst conceived the computational study and did
the implementations, performed data analysis, prepared figures and tables, and
wrote most parts of the manuscript. The former also formulated all properties
and formal proofs stated in the manuscript. Sandra Litz contributed the original
stock price prediction study and Olivia Michael the original robotic soccer
games study.
